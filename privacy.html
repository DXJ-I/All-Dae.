Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

All Dae. is made by Adedeji Bakare ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The same text is in the app under Settings > About > Privacy policy.

The short version

No account, advertising, analytics or tracking. Your diary is stored on your iPhone and we never receive it.

On your iPhone

Food, water, weight, measurements, workouts, habits, fasting, cycle data, progress photos, routes and settings are stored on your iPhone, and on Apple Watch while a workout is in progress, protected by iOS data protection.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your diary and, separately, progress photos sync through your private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. We cannot access it. Challenges and Household share what you add (the name you type, challenge totals, planned meals and shopping items) only with people you invite by link. Data read from Apple Health is never synced to iCloud.

Apple Health

With your permission, All Dae. reads and writes only the data types you allow. Health data is never used for advertising and is never sent to us. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Food search and recipes

Food searches send only your search text or barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). When you import a recipe link, the page is downloaded directly from that website. Meal photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded or saved.

Apple Intelligence

The coach and suggestions run on your iPhone. If a future version offers Apple Private Cloud and you choose it, requests are processed on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which keeps no data.

Diagnostics

Crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share them from Settings.

Children

All Dae. is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings > Your data > Delete all data removes everything All Dae. stores on your iPhone. Manage iCloud data in the Settings app under your name, then iCloud. Manage Health data in the Health app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
